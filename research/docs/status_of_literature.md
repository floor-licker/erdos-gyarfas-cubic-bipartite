# Dated literature status

Audit date: 29 July 2026.

The paper gives two comparisons, both qualified by “to my knowledge”:

- the established published lower bound applicable to cubic-bipartite
  counterexamples is raised from 30 to 60; and
- relative to the newest public exact minimum-degree-three computation, the
  applicable bound is raised from 32 to 60.

The first comparison uses Markström's 2004 cubic computation together with
the accessible 2011 abstract explicitly stating a bipartite lower bound of
30. The second uses Arjun Balaji's public SAT/SMS repository, whose recorded
3 July 2026 state excludes minimum-degree-three graphs through order 31.

The report deliberately does not claim that no unpublished computation or
uncatalogued census reaches the same range. Bibliographic details and access
dates are in `paper/main.tex`.
